## Intro Script (read by Vijay)

Welcome to the show. This is general information, not personal advice — always talk to your own advisor before making financial decisions.

I'm Vijay Stevens, and I'm a tax professional here in Canada. And this is Maya — my co-host, an AI built to ask the questions everyday Canadians are too embarrassed to ask.

Maya's twenty-six, works as an office coordinator in Scarborough, and she's figuring out her own money in real time — credit card debt, a TFSA she doesn't fully understand, a wedding coming up, and parents in retirement she's starting to worry about. She didn't grow up in a house where money was talked about, so she reads the blogs, follows the news, and still has questions. A lot of them.

That's the point of this show. We talk about kitchen-table Canadian finance — RRSPs, TFSAs, FHSAs, tax brackets, the stuff that actually shows up on your CRA notice of assessment. No jargon without translation. No lectures. Just honest questions and real answers.

So if you've ever stared at a tax form and thought, okay, this is probably a dumb question, but... — you're in the right place.

Let's get into it.
