## Quotation and citation

Brief quotations of finance-forge.ai content — for example, in a personal blog,
a Reddit post, an article, or a presentation — are welcome with attribution
back to the relevant page on this site. A simple link is enough; a citation
in the form *"finance-forge.ai — [page title]"* is appreciated.

## Reproduction

Wholesale reproduction of pages, including republishing data tables or
calculator outputs as your own content, is not permitted without written
permission. The point of the site is to be the canonical reference; copies
that diverge from current rates do more harm than good.

## Tax figures and other facts

Numeric figures (tax brackets, contribution limits, CPP/EI rates) are not
copyrightable in themselves and are republished from CRA, Statistics Canada,
and provincial sources. Cite the underlying authority when possible — we do
the same.

## Linking

You may link to any page on finance-forge.ai freely. We prefer that links
point to the canonical page URL rather than to data files or images directly.

## Commercial use

For commercial reuse — for example, embedding our calculator on another
property, or syndicating articles — please reach out via the
[contact page](/contact).
