Each province and territory publishes its own annual budget on a different
calendar. Below are the official budget pages — refer to these for definitive
text on any provincial tax change.

The rate-table impact of provincial budgets (brackets, BPA, surtaxes, credits)
flows into the per-province pages and the calculator via our versioned data
files; this page focuses on the source documents and broader policy notes.
