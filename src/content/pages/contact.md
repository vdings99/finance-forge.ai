The fastest way to reach us is by email. For general questions, corrections, or
suggestions, please use the [contact form on the About page](/about#contact).

We read every message but cannot provide personalised tax, investment, or legal
advice — for that please consult a qualified accountant, financial advisor, or
solicitor.

**Response time:** A few business days, typically. The site is run by a CPA in
between client work, so responses are not instant.

**For media or partnership enquiries:** please mention "media" in the subject
line so the message routes appropriately.
