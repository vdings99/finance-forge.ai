A short, curated list of the authoritative sources behind the figures on this
site. When in doubt, go to the primary source.

## Federal — Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)

- [CRA — Canada.ca tax homepage](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html) — federal personal and business tax
- [CRA — Tax packages](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years.html) — annual tax forms and guides, by year
- [CRA — Indexation adjustment](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/frequently-asked-questions-individuals/adjustment-personal-income-tax-benefit-amounts.html) — the inflation factor that moves brackets each January
- [CRA — Benefit payment dates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/benefit-payment-dates.html) — CCB, GST/HST credit, Climate Action Incentive

## Federal — Department of Finance Canada

- [Department of Finance — Federal Budget](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/services/publications/federal-budget.html) — the annual budget document and supporting backgrounders
- [Department of Finance — Tax expenditure reports](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/services/publications/federal-tax-expenditures.html)

## Statistics and data

- [Statistics Canada](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/) — official source for the Consumer Price Index used to compute indexation factors
- [Bank of Canada — Inflation Calculator](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/related/inflation-calculator/) — handy for adjusting historical figures
