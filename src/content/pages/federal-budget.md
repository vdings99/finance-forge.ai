The federal budget sets the year's tax-policy direction — bracket adjustments,
new credits, changes to registered accounts, and changes to programs like CPP
and OAS. We publish a plain-language summary of each budget here as soon as the
relevant figures clear our review process.

## How to use this page

The headline numbers — brackets, BPA, CPP/EI maximums, RRSP and TFSA limits —
are republished on the dedicated rate pages and used by our calculator. This
page focuses on policy changes that don't fit neatly into a rate table:
new credits, structural changes, transitional rules.

## Official sources

For the source documents, the Department of Finance Canada publishes:

- The [annual federal budget document](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/services/publications/federal-budget.html)
- Supporting backgrounders and tax measures supplements
- The [fall economic statement](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/fall-economic-statement.html)

For the act-level text of any change, search the
[Department of Justice Laws website](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/) for the
*Income Tax Act* or relevant statute.
