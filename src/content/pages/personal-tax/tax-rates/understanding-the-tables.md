Tax rate tables can be confusing if you are not familiar with how progressive taxation works. The marginal rate shown for a bracket applies only to income within that bracket — not to your entire income. Your effective (average) tax rate is always lower than your top marginal rate.

This guide explains how to read the tables on this site and how to calculate your approximate tax using the bracket structure.
