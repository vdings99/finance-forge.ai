## Highest and lowest, by income type

### Employment / Other income
- **Lowest:** Nunavut at 44.50%, then Northwest Territories at 47.05%
- **Highest:** Newfoundland & Labrador at 54.80%, then Nova Scotia at 54.00%
- **Range:** A 10.3 percentage-point spread across the country

### Capital gains
Capital gains rates are always exactly half the "other income" rate, because the 50% inclusion
rate operates identically in every province. The spread is 22.25% (NU) to 27.40% (NL).

### Eligible dividends
The most striking variation: from **28.33%** (NWT) to **46.20%** (NL). In lower brackets,
eligible dividends can produce a **negative** marginal rate — the dividend tax credit exceeds
the gross-up tax, creating a net refund on that income. See the [full bracket table](/personal-tax/tax-rates/marginal-rates) for bracket-by-bracket negative rates.

### Non-eligible dividends
Range: 36.82% (NWT) to 49.99% (NS). Non-eligible dividends are taxed less favourably than
eligible dividends because the smaller gross-up produces a smaller credit — these dividends
originate from income taxed at the lower small-business corporate rate.

## Why do rates differ so much between provinces?

The federal component is the same everywhere ($57,375 bracket is 14.5% in 2025 for all provinces except Quebec where the federal abatement applies). The difference is entirely the **provincial rate** added on top.

Provinces with higher social spending (NL, NS, QC) have opted for higher personal income
tax rates. Resource-heavy provinces (AB, SK) tend toward lower personal rates, partly
offset by resource royalty revenues.

## How bracket thresholds work

The $253,414 figure appearing in most provinces is the **top federal bracket threshold** for 2025.
Once a province's own top bracket also kicks in at or before that income level, the combined
top rate applies from $253,414 onward.

Exceptions:
- **Alberta** — top provincial bracket begins at $362,961 (higher than the federal top)
- **Yukon** — top rate doesn't apply until $500,000 due to Yukon's unique graduated schedule
- **Newfoundland & Labrador** — multiple ultra-high-income brackets extend beyond $1.1M
- **British Columbia** — top bracket kicks in at $259,829 (a B.C.-specific threshold slightly above the federal top)
