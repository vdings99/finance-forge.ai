Understanding the full calculation chain from gross income to net tax payable is essential for effective tax planning. The process involves several distinct steps: calculating total income, subtracting deductions to arrive at net income and then taxable income, applying graduated tax rates, and then reducing the tax by non-refundable and refundable credits.
