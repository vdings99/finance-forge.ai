The total tax you pay on the same income can vary by thousands of dollars depending on which province or territory you reside in. These differences arise from varying provincial tax rates, bracket thresholds, surtaxes, and available credits.

This page compares the total combined federal and provincial tax payable at representative income levels across all 13 jurisdictions.
