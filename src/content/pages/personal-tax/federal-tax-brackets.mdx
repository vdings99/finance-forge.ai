## 2025 federal brackets

Federal personal income tax in 2025 uses five brackets, the lowest taxed at
15% and the top taxed at 33%. These figures are republished from our
[versioned tax data](/statistics) and feed the
[Detailed Income Tax Calculator](/calculators/income-tax) directly.

<TaxBracketTable year={2025} jurisdiction="federal" />

The 2025 federal basic personal amount is $16,129 (the credit reduces federal
tax by 15% × $16,129 ≈ $2,419 for most taxpayers).

## Combined federal and provincial — Ontario example

Outside Quebec, your total marginal tax rate is the federal rate at your
income plus the provincial rate at your income. Ontario provincial brackets:

<TaxBracketTable year={2025} jurisdiction="ON" caption="2025 Ontario Personal Income Tax Brackets" />

Adding the federal rate to the Ontario rate at each income level produces the
following combined marginal rates (Ontario surtaxes not yet applied, basic
personal amount credits not netted):

| Taxable income | Federal | Ontario | Combined |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Up to $51,446 | 15.0% | 5.05% | 20.05% |
| $51,446 – $57,375 | 15.0% | 9.15% | 24.15% |
| $57,375 – $102,894 | 20.5% | 9.15% | 29.65% |
| $102,894 – $114,750 | 20.5% | 11.16% | 31.66% |
| $114,750 – $150,000 | 26.0% | 11.16% | 37.16% |
| $150,000 – $158,468 | 26.0% | 12.16% | 38.16% |
| $158,468 – $220,000 | 29.0% | 12.16% | 41.16% |
| Over $220,000 | 33.0% | 13.16% | 46.16% |

<Callout type="warning">
The combined rates above do not include the **Ontario surtax**, which adds 20% to provincial tax above $5,554 and a further 36% above $7,108 in 2025. The surtax has the effect of raising the top marginal rate at higher incomes; the [Detailed Income Tax Calculator](/calculators/income-tax) applies it correctly.
</Callout>
