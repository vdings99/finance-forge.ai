Planning for retirement requires a clear picture of all your income sources. This calculator helps you project your total annual retirement income by combining government benefits, employer pensions, registered savings withdrawals, and other investment income.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Expected CPP retirement pension** — monthly amount based on your contribution history
- **Expected OAS pension** — monthly Old Age Security amount
- **Employer pension income** — defined benefit or defined contribution pension amount
- **RRSP/RRIF withdrawals** — planned annual withdrawals from registered savings
- **Other investment income** — dividends, interest, rental income
- **Desired retirement age** — when you plan to begin drawing income
