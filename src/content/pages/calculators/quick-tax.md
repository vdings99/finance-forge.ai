For a quick check — "what's the rough tax on $90,000 in BC this year?" — the
full Detailed Calculator's grouped inputs are overkill. This calculator pares
the interface down to a single income field and a province selector, and shows
just federal tax, provincial tax, CPP, EI, total, and effective rate.

The figures it returns are identical to what the
[Detailed Income Tax Calculator](/calculators/income-tax) would return for
employment-only income with no deductions or credits.

**Preview** — the interface is shown here for reference. The engine that powers
the Detailed Calculator will be wired in here too; expect this as one of the
first preview shells to activate.
