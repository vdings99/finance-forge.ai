The Employee Tools & Deductions Calculator helps salaried and commissioned employees determine which work-related expenses they can claim on their Canadian tax return. Enter your employment expenses below to see an estimate of your allowable deductions under CRA guidelines.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Employment income** — your total T4 employment income for the tax year
- **Tools purchased** — cost of eligible tools required for employment
- **Supplies and materials** — amounts spent on supplies consumed in your duties
- **Professional dues** — annual professional membership dues required by your employer
- **Other allowable expenses** — additional CRA-eligible employment expenses
