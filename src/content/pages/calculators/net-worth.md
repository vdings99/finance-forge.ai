Your net worth is the difference between what you own and what you owe. Tracking it over time is one of the most effective ways to measure your financial progress. This calculator helps you organize your assets and liabilities into a clear summary.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Cash and bank accounts** — chequing, savings, GICs
- **Registered accounts** — RRSP, TFSA, RESP, FHSA balances
- **Non-registered investments** — stocks, bonds, mutual funds
- **Real estate** — market value of properties owned
- **Other assets** — vehicles, business interests, valuables
- **Mortgages** — outstanding mortgage balances
- **Other debts** — credit cards, lines of credit, student loans, car loans
