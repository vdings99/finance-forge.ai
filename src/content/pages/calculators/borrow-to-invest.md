Leveraged investing — borrowing to invest in a non-registered account — can amplify both gains and losses. The interest on money borrowed to earn investment income is generally tax-deductible in Canada. This calculator helps you model the after-tax outcome of a leveraged investment strategy.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Amount borrowed** — the principal of the investment loan
- **Loan interest rate** — annual rate on the investment loan
- **Expected investment return** — anticipated annual return on the portfolio
- **Investment income type** — dividends, interest, or capital gains
- **Marginal tax rate** — your combined federal/provincial rate
- **Investment time horizon** — number of years
