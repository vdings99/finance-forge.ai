Understanding the time value of money is fundamental to financial planning. This calculator lets you determine either how much a future payment is worth in today's dollars (present value) or how much a current investment will grow to over time (future value) at a given rate of return.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Calculation type** — present value or future value
- **Dollar amount** — the known value (present or future)
- **Annual interest/discount rate** — the rate used for compounding or discounting
- **Number of years** — the time period
- **Compounding frequency** — annually, semi-annually, quarterly, or monthly
