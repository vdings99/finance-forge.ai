The RRSP and RRIF are two phases of the same registered account: you contribute
to and grow an RRSP during your working years, then convert it to a RRIF (or
annuity) by the end of the year you turn 71 and begin mandatory minimum
withdrawals.

This calculator covers both phases. On the contribution side it computes your
deduction limit and the marginal tax saved by contributing. On the withdrawal
side it computes the minimum required withdrawal each year using CRA's
age-based factors and projects after-tax retirement income.
