Quebec is the one province where personal income tax is not administered by
the Canada Revenue Agency — Revenu Québec handles it directly, with its own
return (TP-1) and its own brackets. Because the rules diverge in several
material ways from the rest-of-Canada system used by our
[Detailed Income Tax Calculator](/calculators/income-tax), Quebec gets a
dedicated calculator.

**Preview** — the interface is shown here for reference. The Quebec calculation
engine is in development and will be activated in a future update; until then,
please use Revenu Québec's [own calculator](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/online-services/online-services-and-forms-by-topic/online-services/services-for-individuals-and-individuals-in-business/).
