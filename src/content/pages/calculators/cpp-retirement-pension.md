Your CPP retirement pension depends on how much and how long you contributed, and when you choose to start receiving it. Starting before age 65 reduces your pension permanently, while deferring to age 70 increases it. This calculator estimates your monthly and annual CPP retirement pension.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Age to start CPP** — between 60 and 70
- **Years of maximum contributions** — how many years you contributed at the maximum level
- **Average earnings as percentage of YMPE** — your career average relative to the Year's Maximum Pensionable Earnings
- **Current age** — your age today
