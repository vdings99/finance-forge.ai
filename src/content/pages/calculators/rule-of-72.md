The Rule of 72 is a simple formula that estimates how many years it takes for an investment to double in value at a fixed annual rate of return. Divide 72 by the annual return percentage to get an approximate doubling time. This calculator applies the rule instantly.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Annual rate of return** — the expected fixed annual return percentage
- **Initial investment** — optional starting amount for illustration
