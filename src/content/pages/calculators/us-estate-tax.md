Canadian residents who own US-situs property — including US real estate, US stocks held directly, and other US assets — may be subject to US estate tax on death. This calculator provides an estimate of potential US estate tax exposure based on the Canada-US Tax Treaty provisions.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Total worldwide estate value** — fair market value of all assets worldwide
- **US-situs assets value** — fair market value of US real estate, US equities, and other US property
- **Filing status** — single, married, or surviving spouse
- **Canadian taxes payable on US assets** — for treaty credit calculations
