Canadian tax law treats investment income asymmetrically. Interest income is
fully included; eligible Canadian dividends get a gross-up and a federal +
provincial dividend tax credit; capital gains are included at 50%; and foreign
dividends are taxed at the same rate as interest with foreign tax credits
applied separately.

For a non-trivial portfolio, the effective tax rate on $10,000 of interest can
be more than double the effective rate on $10,000 of eligible dividends. This
calculator visualises that spread for any combination of income types and
province.
