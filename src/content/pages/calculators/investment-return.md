The investment-return calculator answers the question "if I save $X per year
at Y% over Z years, what will I have?" — and crucially, it answers it in both
nominal and real (after-inflation) dollars. Mortgage-amortisation logic for
debt-side compounding is on the [Loan & Mortgage Calculator](/calculators/loan-mortgage).

**Preview** — the interface is shown here for reference. The calculation engine
is in development. Standard compound-growth formulas mean this is among the
easiest of our preview shells to activate; expect it in an early update.
