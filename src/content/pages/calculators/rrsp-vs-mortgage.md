Should you put extra cash toward your RRSP or pay down your mortgage faster? The answer depends on your mortgage rate, investment returns, and tax bracket. This calculator compares both strategies over your chosen time horizon so you can see which approach builds more wealth.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Extra amount available** — monthly dollars available for either strategy
- **Mortgage interest rate** — your current mortgage rate
- **Remaining mortgage balance** — outstanding principal
- **Remaining amortization** — years left on your mortgage
- **Expected RRSP return** — annual rate of return inside the RRSP
- **Marginal tax rate** — your combined federal/provincial rate
