## What "preview shell" means

Several calculators on the site are currently interface-only: the input
fields, layout, and result panels are wired up, but the calculation engine is
still in development. Only the **Detailed Income Tax Calculator** is currently
live. The preview shells will be activated one at a time; see
[What's New](/whats-new) for announcements.
