Borrowing to contribute to your RRSP can be a smart strategy if the tax refund and investment growth outweigh the loan interest costs. This calculator helps you evaluate whether an RRSP loan makes sense by comparing the net benefit of the contribution against the cost of borrowing.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **RRSP contribution amount** — the amount you plan to borrow and contribute
- **Marginal tax rate** — your current combined federal/provincial rate
- **Loan interest rate** — the annual rate on the RRSP loan
- **Loan repayment period** — months to repay the loan
- **Expected investment return** — annual rate of return inside the RRSP
