Choosing between a TFSA and an RRSP depends on your current marginal tax rate, your expected tax rate in retirement, and your investment time horizon. This calculator compares the after-tax value of each option so you can make an informed decision about where to direct your savings.

> **Note:** This calculator interface is coming soon. The calculation engine is being developed and will be available in a future update.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Amount to invest** — the pre-tax dollars available
- **Current marginal tax rate** — your top combined federal/provincial rate today
- **Expected marginal tax rate in retirement** — your projected rate when withdrawing
- **Investment return rate** — expected annual rate of return
- **Investment time horizon** — number of years until withdrawal
