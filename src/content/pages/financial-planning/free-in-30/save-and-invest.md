Once you have established an emergency fund and eliminated high-interest debt, the next step is to put your money to work through disciplined, long-term investing. In Canada, registered accounts like the TFSA and RRSP provide significant tax advantages that accelerate wealth accumulation when used strategically.

The key principles of successful long-term investing are diversification, low costs, and consistency. Broadly diversified index funds or ETFs held in tax-advantaged accounts, with regular automatic contributions, have historically outperformed the vast majority of actively managed strategies over periods of 20 years or more.
