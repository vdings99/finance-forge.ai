The Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) allows Canadian residents aged 18 and older to earn investment income — including interest, dividends, and capital gains — completely free of tax. Unlike an RRSP, contributions are made with after-tax dollars, but withdrawals are entirely tax-free and do not affect income-tested government benefits.

The annual TFSA contribution limit for 2025 is $7,000, with a cumulative lifetime limit of $102,000 for anyone who has been eligible since the program launched in 2009. Unused contribution room carries forward indefinitely, and amounts withdrawn in one year are added back to your contribution room in the following year.
