The Registered Education Savings Plan (RESP) is a tax-sheltered savings account designed to help Canadian families save for a child's post-secondary education. Contributions are not tax-deductible, but investment growth is tax-sheltered, and the federal government provides matching grants through the Canada Education Savings Grant (CESG) — effectively a 20% immediate return on the first $2,500 contributed each year per beneficiary.

The lifetime contribution limit per beneficiary is $50,000, and the CESG provides up to $7,200 in total grants per child. Low-income families may also qualify for the Canada Learning Bond (CLB), which provides up to $2,000 in additional government contributions without requiring any family contributions at all.
