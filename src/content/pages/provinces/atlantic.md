## New Brunswick

New Brunswick levies personal income tax in addition to federal tax. The province uses a four-bracket system.

## Newfoundland and Labrador

Newfoundland and Labrador has one of the most progressive provincial tax systems in Canada, with eight tax brackets reaching up to the highest provincial rates.

## Nova Scotia

Nova Scotia's five-bracket system includes among the highest top marginal rates in Atlantic Canada.

[View Nova Scotia tax information →](/provinces/ns)

## Prince Edward Island

Prince Edward Island applies a provincial surtax on higher provincial tax amounts in addition to its three-bracket income tax.
