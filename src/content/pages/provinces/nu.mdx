Nunavut has the lowest provincial/territorial tax rates in Canada at the bottom bracket (4%) and uses only the federal GST.

## Personal income tax rates

Nunavut has 4 territorial tax brackets in 2025. The territorial basic personal amount is **$17,925**, which reduces tax by 4.00% of the amount (the first-bracket rate).

## Tax credits

In addition to the federal non-refundable credits, Nunavut offers its own set of territorial credits — including the territorial basic personal amount and (in most provinces) a territorial dividend tax credit, shown in our [Non-Refundable Tax Credits](/personal-tax/non-refundable-credits) page. Jurisdiction-specific credits (such as caregiver and home-renovation credits) are administered separately; see the official Nunavut resources at the bottom of this page.

## Sales and property tax

**Sales tax in Nunavut**: GST only (5%) — no territorial sales tax.

Property tax is administered at the municipal level in Nunavut and varies by city or municipality. The territory does not collect a residential property tax directly.

## Government benefits

Most federal benefits available across Canada — Canada Child Benefit, GST/HST credit, Climate Action Incentive Payment (where applicable), and OAS/CPP — apply in Nunavut the same way as in any other jurisdiction. Nunavut also administers its own credits and benefits; consult the territorial finance page below for current programs.

For a Canada-wide summary see our [Government Benefits & Services](/financial-planning/government-benefits) page.

## Budgets and fiscal plans

The Nunavut budget is published annually. For the most recent budget document and supporting materials, refer to the [Nunavut Budget page](https://www.gov.nu.ca/finance).
