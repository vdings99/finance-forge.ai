Quebec is the one province that administers its **own personal income tax** separately from CRA. Residents file two returns each year (the federal T1 and the provincial TP-1). The federal calculations on this site apply with the Quebec abatement; the provincial side uses Quebec's own brackets and credit system.

## Personal income tax rates

Quebec's provincial personal income tax is administered by **Revenu Québec**, not CRA, with its own brackets and credit structure. Our [Detailed Income Tax Calculator](/calculators/income-tax) does not currently cover Quebec — a [dedicated Quebec calculator](/calculators/quebec-tax) is in development. For current Quebec brackets, please refer directly to the [Revenu Québec rate tables](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/income-tax-return/completing-your-income-tax-return/income-tax-rates/).

## Tax credits

In addition to the federal non-refundable credits, Quebec offers its own set of provincial credits — including the provincial basic personal amount and (in most provinces) a provincial dividend tax credit, shown in our [Non-Refundable Tax Credits](/personal-tax/non-refundable-credits) page. Jurisdiction-specific credits (such as caregiver and home-renovation credits) are administered separately; see the official Quebec resources at the bottom of this page.

## Sales and property tax

**Sales tax in Quebec**: GST 5% + QST 9.975% (Quebec Sales Tax, administered by Revenu Québec).

Property tax is administered at the municipal level in Quebec and varies by city or municipality. The province does not collect a residential property tax directly.

## Government benefits

Most federal benefits available across Canada — Canada Child Benefit, GST/HST credit, Climate Action Incentive Payment (where applicable), and OAS/CPP — apply in Quebec the same way as in any other jurisdiction. Quebec also administers its own credits and benefits; consult the provincial finance page below for current programs.

For a Canada-wide summary see our [Government Benefits & Services](/financial-planning/government-benefits) page.

## Budgets and fiscal plans

The Quebec budget is published annually. For the most recent budget document and supporting materials, refer to the [Quebec Budget page](https://www.budget.finances.gouv.qc.ca/).
