New Brunswick uses the Harmonized Sales Tax (HST) which combines the federal GST and provincial sales tax into a single 15% rate. The province has a progressive personal income tax system with rates that are moderate compared to other Atlantic provinces.

## Personal Tax Rates

New Brunswick's personal tax rates range from 9.40% to 19.50%. View the complete [NB tax rate tables](/personal-tax/tax-rates) for current brackets and combined federal-provincial marginal rates.

## Tax Credits

New Brunswick provides the NB Low-Income Tax Reduction, the NB Tuition Rebate for graduates, and other provincial credits.

## Sales and Property Taxes

New Brunswick charges a combined HST of 15% (5% federal + 10% provincial). Property taxes are levied by the province based on assessed property values.

## Government Benefits

New Brunswick offers the NB Child Tax Benefit, NB Working Income Supplement, and other programs that complement federal benefits.
