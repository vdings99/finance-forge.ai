Newfoundland and Labrador has among the highest combined marginal tax rates in Canada and uses the Harmonized Sales Tax. The province has made efforts to reduce tax burdens in recent budgets while maintaining essential public services.

## Personal Tax Rates

NL's personal tax rates range from 8.7% to 21.8%, with a temporary additional levy on high incomes. View the complete [NL tax rate tables](/personal-tax/tax-rates) for current brackets and combined federal-provincial marginal rates.

## Tax Credits

NL offers several provincial credits including the Newfoundland and Labrador Income Supplement and the Seniors' Benefit.

## Sales and Property Taxes

NL charges a combined HST of 15% (5% federal + 10% provincial). Municipal property taxes vary by community.

## Government Benefits

NL provides the NL Income Supplement, NL Seniors' Benefit, and the NL Child Benefit to support lower-income residents.
