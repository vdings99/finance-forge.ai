Yukon uses only the federal GST and offers a unique Yukon First Nations Tax Credit for residents of certain settled First Nations lands.

## Personal income tax rates

Yukon has 5 territorial tax brackets in 2025. The territorial basic personal amount is **$16,129**, which reduces tax by 6.40% of the amount (the first-bracket rate).

## Tax credits

In addition to the federal non-refundable credits, Yukon offers its own set of territorial credits — including the territorial basic personal amount and (in most provinces) a territorial dividend tax credit, shown in our [Non-Refundable Tax Credits](/personal-tax/non-refundable-credits) page. Jurisdiction-specific credits (such as caregiver and home-renovation credits) are administered separately; see the official Yukon resources at the bottom of this page.

## Sales and property tax

**Sales tax in Yukon**: GST only (5%) — no territorial sales tax.

Property tax is administered at the municipal level in Yukon and varies by city or municipality. The territory does not collect a residential property tax directly.

## Government benefits

Most federal benefits available across Canada — Canada Child Benefit, GST/HST credit, Climate Action Incentive Payment (where applicable), and OAS/CPP — apply in Yukon the same way as in any other jurisdiction. Yukon also administers its own credits and benefits; consult the territorial finance page below for current programs.

For a Canada-wide summary see our [Government Benefits & Services](/financial-planning/government-benefits) page.

## Budgets and fiscal plans

The Yukon budget is published annually. For the most recent budget document and supporting materials, refer to the [Yukon Budget page](https://yukon.ca/en/finance).
