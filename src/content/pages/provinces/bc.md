British Columbia has a multi-bracket progressive income tax system and levies a separate Provincial Sales Tax (PST) in addition to the federal GST. The province also has unique measures like the BC speculation and vacancy tax targeting housing affordability.

## Personal Tax Rates

BC has one of the most graduated personal income tax systems in Canada, with rates ranging from 5.06% to 20.5%. View the complete [BC tax rate tables](/personal-tax/tax-rates) for current brackets and combined federal-provincial marginal rates.

## Tax Credits

BC offers the BC Climate Action Tax Credit, the BC Training Tax Credit, and other provincial credits to reduce the tax burden on residents.

## Sales and Property Taxes

BC charges a 7% PST on most goods and some services, in addition to the 5% federal GST. The province also levies property transfer tax on real estate purchases and has a speculation tax in designated areas.

## Government Benefits

BC provides the BC Climate Action Tax Credit, BC Family Benefit, and BC Seniors' Supplement to support lower-income residents and families.
