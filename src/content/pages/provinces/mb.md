Manitoba uses a progressive personal income tax system and charges a Retail Sales Tax (RST) on most goods and services. The province has historically maintained moderate tax rates relative to other Canadian jurisdictions.

## Personal Tax Rates

Manitoba's personal tax rates range from 10.8% to 17.4%. View the complete [Manitoba tax rate tables](/personal-tax/tax-rates) for current brackets and combined federal-provincial marginal rates.

## Tax Credits

Manitoba provides several provincial credits including the Manitoba Personal Tax Credit, the Primary Caregiver Tax Credit, and education-related credits.

## Sales and Property Taxes

Manitoba charges a 7% RST on most goods and designated services, in addition to the 5% federal GST. Property taxes are assessed by municipal governments.

## Government Benefits

Manitoba offers provincial benefit programs including the Manitoba Child Benefit and various tax credit programs for seniors and families.
