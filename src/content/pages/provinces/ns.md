Nova Scotia has the highest combined top marginal tax rate in Canada and uses the Harmonized Sales Tax at 15%. The province offers several targeted tax credits and benefit programs to offset the higher tax burden for lower- and middle-income residents.

## Personal Tax Rates

NS personal tax rates range from 8.79% to 21.0%. View the complete [NS tax rate tables](/personal-tax/tax-rates) for current brackets and combined federal-provincial marginal rates.

## Tax Credits

NS provides the NS Affordable Living Tax Credit, the Poverty Reduction Credit, and credits for volunteer firefighters and search-and-rescue volunteers.

## Sales and Property Taxes

NS charges a combined HST of 15% (5% federal + 10% provincial). Municipal property taxes are set by individual municipalities.

## Government Benefits

NS offers the NS Affordable Living Tax Credit and the NS Child Benefit to support families and lower-income residents.
