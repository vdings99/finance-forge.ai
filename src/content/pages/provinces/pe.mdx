Prince Edward Island uses HST in place of separate GST/PST and applies a small provincial surtax on higher provincial-tax amounts.

## Personal income tax rates

Prince Edward Island has 5 provincial tax brackets in 2025. The provincial basic personal amount is **$12,000**, which reduces tax by 9.60% of the amount (the first-bracket rate). The province applies a **surtax** on higher provincial tax amounts.

## Tax credits

In addition to the federal non-refundable credits, Prince Edward Island offers its own set of provincial credits — including the provincial basic personal amount and (in most provinces) a provincial dividend tax credit, shown in our [Non-Refundable Tax Credits](/personal-tax/non-refundable-credits) page. Jurisdiction-specific credits (such as caregiver and home-renovation credits) are administered separately; see the official Prince Edward Island resources at the bottom of this page.

## Sales and property tax

**Sales tax in Prince Edward Island**: HST at 15% (combined federal GST 5% + provincial 10%).

Property tax is administered at the municipal level in Prince Edward Island and varies by city or municipality. The province does not collect a residential property tax directly.

## Government benefits

Most federal benefits available across Canada — Canada Child Benefit, GST/HST credit, Climate Action Incentive Payment (where applicable), and OAS/CPP — apply in Prince Edward Island the same way as in any other jurisdiction. Prince Edward Island also administers its own credits and benefits; consult the provincial finance page below for current programs.

For a Canada-wide summary see our [Government Benefits & Services](/financial-planning/government-benefits) page.
