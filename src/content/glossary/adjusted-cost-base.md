---
term: "Adjusted Cost Base"
letter: "A"
definition: "The original cost of an investment plus any additional costs such as commissions and fees, used to calculate capital gains or losses on disposition."
seeAlso: ["capital-gain", "capital-loss"]
revised: "2026-05-30"
---

The **adjusted cost base (ACB)** is the original cost of a property or investment, adjusted for certain additions or deductions allowed by the *Income Tax Act*. When you sell a property, the capital gain or loss is calculated as the proceeds of disposition minus the ACB and any selling expenses.

For shares purchased at different times, the ACB is calculated as the total cost of all identical shares divided by the number of shares held (the averaging rule). Reinvested dividends and return-of-capital distributions can also affect the ACB.
