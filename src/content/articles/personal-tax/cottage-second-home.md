## Tax Considerations for Cottage Owners

When you own both a primary home and a cottage, only one property can be designated as your principal residence for any given tax year. This means the capital gain on the other property will be partially or fully taxable. Strategic designation of the principal residence exemption can save tens of thousands of dollars in tax.
