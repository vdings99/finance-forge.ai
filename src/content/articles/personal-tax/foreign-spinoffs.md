## Foreign Spinoff Tax Rules for Canadians

When a US or other foreign corporation distributes shares of a subsidiary to its shareholders (a spinoff), Canadian investors face potential tax consequences. Without a qualifying election under section 86.1 of the Income Tax Act, the fair market value of the spinoff shares received may be treated as a taxable dividend — even though no cash was received.
