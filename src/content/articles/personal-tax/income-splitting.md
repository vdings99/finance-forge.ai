## Overview of Income Splitting

Canada's progressive tax system means a family where one spouse earns $200,000 pays significantly more tax than a family where two spouses each earn $100,000. Income splitting strategies attempt to distribute income more evenly among family members to take advantage of lower marginal rates and unused credits.
