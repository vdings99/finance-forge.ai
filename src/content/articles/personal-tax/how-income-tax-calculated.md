## How the Canadian Tax System Works

Canada uses a progressive, graduated tax system where your income is taxed at increasing rates as it rises through defined brackets. Your total income from all sources is first reduced by allowable deductions to arrive at taxable income, and then non-refundable tax credits reduce the tax otherwise payable.
