## Using Non-Capital Losses

When your allowable business deductions exceed your business income, the result is a non-capital loss. This loss can be applied against other sources of income in the current year, carried back to the three preceding tax years, or carried forward up to 20 years to offset future taxable income, providing valuable tax relief during profitable periods.
