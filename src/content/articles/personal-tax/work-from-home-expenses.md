## Home Office Expense Deductions

If you work from home and your employer requires it as a condition of employment, you may be eligible to claim home office expenses using the detailed method. This requires a signed T2200 from your employer and allows you to deduct a proportionate share of rent, utilities, internet, and maintenance costs based on the size and use of your workspace.
