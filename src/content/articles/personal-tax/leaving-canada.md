## Tax Consequences of Leaving Canada

When you cease to be a Canadian tax resident, the Income Tax Act treats you as having sold most of your property at fair market value immediately before departure. This deemed disposition can trigger capital gains on investments, real estate (other than your principal residence), and other property.
