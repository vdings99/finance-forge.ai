## Reading Your T4 Slip

Every Canadian employee receives a T4 slip from each employer by the end of February following the tax year. The slip contains numbered boxes reporting specific amounts — Box 14 shows your total employment income, Box 22 shows income tax deducted, and other boxes detail CPP contributions, EI premiums, union dues, and taxable benefits.
