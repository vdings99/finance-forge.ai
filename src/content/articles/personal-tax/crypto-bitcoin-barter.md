## Tax Treatment of Cryptocurrency in Canada

When you dispose of cryptocurrency — by selling, trading, gifting, or using it to purchase goods or services — you must report the resulting capital gain or loss, or business income, on your Canadian tax return. The CRA considers each disposal a taxable event, and you must track the adjusted cost base of every holding.
