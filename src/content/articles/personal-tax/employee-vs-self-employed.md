## Employee or Self-Employed?

The CRA uses several factors to determine your work status, including the degree of control over how work is performed, ownership of tools, financial risk, and the opportunity for profit. Misclassification can result in reassessments, penalties, and unexpected tax liabilities for both workers and payers.
