## Claiming Medical Expenses

The federal medical expense tax credit (METC) allows you to claim eligible medical expenses that exceed the lesser of 3% of your net income or a fixed threshold (indexed annually). You can claim expenses paid during any 12-month period ending in the tax year, giving you flexibility to maximize the credit.
