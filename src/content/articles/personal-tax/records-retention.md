## Record Keeping Requirements

Canadian taxpayers must retain all records and supporting documents needed to verify the information on their tax returns. The standard retention period is six years from the end of the last tax year to which they relate. Certain records — such as those related to capital property — should be kept as long as you own the property plus six years after disposal.
