## Tax Tips for Part-Time Workers

If you work part-time and earn below the basic personal amount, you may not owe any federal income tax at all. However, if your employer does not have a properly completed TD1 form on file, tax may still be withheld from every paycheque. Filing a tax return ensures you receive a refund of any excess tax withheld.
