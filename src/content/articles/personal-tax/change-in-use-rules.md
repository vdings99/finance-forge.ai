## Understanding Change in Use

When a property changes its use — for example, from a principal residence to a rental property, or from a rental to your home — the Income Tax Act deems the property to have been disposed of at its fair market value at the time of the change. This deemed disposition can trigger an immediate capital gain, though elections may be available to defer the tax.
