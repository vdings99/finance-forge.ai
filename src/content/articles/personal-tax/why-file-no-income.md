## Benefits of Filing With Low or No Income

Many Canadians miss out on significant government benefits simply because they do not file a tax return. The GST/HST credit, Canada Child Benefit, Canada Workers Benefit, and various provincial credits all require a filed return to determine eligibility and payment amounts.
