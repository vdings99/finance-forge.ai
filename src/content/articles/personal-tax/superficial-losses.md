## Understanding Superficial Loss Rules

The superficial loss rule prevents investors from selling a security to crystallize a capital loss and then immediately repurchasing the same security. If you, your spouse, or a corporation you control acquires the same or identical property during the period from 30 days before to 30 days after the sale, the capital loss is denied and added to the ACB of the repurchased property.
