## Understanding Shareholder Loan Rules

When a Canadian-controlled private corporation (CCPC) makes a loan to a shareholder or a related person, the loan amount may be included in the shareholder's income for that tax year unless it falls within specific exceptions. The most common exception requires the loan to be repaid within two corporate tax year-ends.
