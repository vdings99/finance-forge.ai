## Tax Treatment of Severance Pay

A retiring allowance — commonly known as severance pay — is fully taxable in the year received. Your employer is required to withhold tax at prescribed rates depending on the amount. However, for years of service before 1996, a portion of the retiring allowance may be directly transferred to your RRSP without affecting your regular contribution room.
