## How TD1 Forms Work

When you start a new job or your personal situation changes, you complete a federal TD1 and a provincial or territorial TD1 form. These forms declare your personal tax credits — such as the basic personal amount, age amount, pension income amount, and tuition credits — which your employer uses to calculate the correct amount of income tax to withhold from each paycheque.
