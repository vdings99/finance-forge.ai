## When Online Selling Becomes Taxable

Occasional sales of personal items at a loss are generally not taxable. However, if you buy goods with the intention of reselling them for profit, or if your selling activity has the characteristics of a business — regular transactions, profit motive, organized operations — the income must be reported.
