## How Attribution Rules Work

When you transfer or loan property to your spouse or common-law partner, any income or loss from that property — and any capital gain or loss on its disposition — is attributed back to you for tax purposes. For transfers to minor children, income (but not capital gains) is attributed back to the transferor until the child turns 18.
