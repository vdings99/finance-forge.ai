## The Principal Residence Exemption

Canada's principal residence exemption (PRE) allows you to shelter some or all of the capital gain realized on the sale of a property that was your principal residence during the years you owned it. Since 2016, you must report the sale on your tax return and designate the property as your principal residence, even if the gain is fully exempt.
