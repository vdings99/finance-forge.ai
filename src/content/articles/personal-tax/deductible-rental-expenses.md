## Rental Expense Deductions

As a Canadian landlord, you can deduct current expenses — those that recur regularly and maintain the property — from your gross rental income. Common deductible expenses include property taxes, insurance, advertising, repairs and maintenance, utilities (if you pay them), and the interest portion of your mortgage payments.
