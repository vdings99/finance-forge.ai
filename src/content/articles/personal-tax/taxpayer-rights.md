## The Taxpayer Bill of Rights

Canada's Taxpayer Bill of Rights outlines the standard of treatment you are entitled to when interacting with the CRA. These rights include being presumed honest unless proven otherwise, receiving clear explanations of CRA decisions, and having access to a formal complaint and review process.
