## Tax Credits and Benefits for Students

Canadian students can claim the tuition tax credit for eligible tuition fees paid to qualifying institutions. Unused tuition credits can be carried forward indefinitely or transferred (up to a limit) to a parent, grandparent, or spouse. Scholarship, fellowship, and bursary income received for enrollment in a qualifying program is generally exempt from tax.
