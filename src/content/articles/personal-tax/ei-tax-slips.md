## EI Benefits and Tax Reporting

All Employment Insurance benefits — including regular benefits, maternity/parental benefits, sickness benefits, and caregiving benefits — are taxable and must be reported on your income tax return. Service Canada issues a T4E slip showing the total benefits paid and the income tax deducted during the year.
