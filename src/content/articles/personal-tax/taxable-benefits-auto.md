## Automobile Taxable Benefits

When an employer provides a vehicle to an employee, two separate taxable benefits may apply: the standby charge (based on the cost of the vehicle or lease payments) and the operating expense benefit (covering fuel, insurance, and maintenance for personal use). The standby charge can be reduced if personal use is less than a specified threshold.
