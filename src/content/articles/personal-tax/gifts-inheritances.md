## Tax Rules for Gifts and Inheritances

While Canada does not impose a specific gift or inheritance tax, the transfer of capital property — whether by gift during your lifetime or by bequest on death — is generally treated as a disposition at fair market value. This means the person giving the gift or the deceased's estate may owe tax on any accrued capital gains.
