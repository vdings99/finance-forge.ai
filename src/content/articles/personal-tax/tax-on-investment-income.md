## Comparing Tax Treatment of Investment Income

Canada taxes the three main types of investment income — interest, dividends, and capital gains — very differently. Interest income is fully included in taxable income at your marginal rate. Eligible Canadian dividends benefit from the dividend tax credit, which significantly reduces the effective tax rate. Capital gains are partially included based on the current inclusion rate.
