## Deducting Interest Expenses

Under paragraph 20(1)(c) of the Income Tax Act, interest paid on money borrowed for the purpose of earning income from a business or property is deductible. This is why interest on an investment loan used to purchase stocks or rental property is generally deductible, while interest on a personal mortgage or car loan is not.
