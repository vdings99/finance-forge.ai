## Claiming the Employee GST/HST Rebate

If you claim employment expenses on form T777 and those expenses included GST/HST, you may be eligible for a rebate of the sales tax portion using form GST370. The rebate is added to your income in the year you receive it, but the net effect is still a benefit because the rebate partially offsets the original expense.
