## Key Year-End Tax Planning Strategies

Year-end tax planning involves reviewing your income, deductions, and credits before December 31 to identify opportunities to minimize your tax liability. Common strategies include maximizing RRSP contributions, triggering capital losses to offset gains, making charitable donations, and timing income or expenses.
