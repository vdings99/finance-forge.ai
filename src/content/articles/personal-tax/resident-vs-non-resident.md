## Determining Your Tax Residency

Canada taxes residents on their worldwide income and non-residents only on Canadian-source income. Your residency status is based on the nature and permanence of your ties to Canada — including where you maintain a home, where your spouse and dependants reside, and the location of your personal property and social connections.
