## Understanding Taxable Benefits

The CRA considers most benefits received from your employer as taxable income. This includes items like employer-paid gym memberships, personal use of company property, gift cards, and cash awards. However, non-cash gifts and awards may be exempt up to $500 per year, and certain long-service awards have a separate $500 exemption.
