## Understanding Capital Gains and Losses

A capital gain occurs when you dispose of a capital property — such as stocks, bonds, mutual funds, or real estate — for more than its adjusted cost base (ACB) plus any outlays and expenses of disposition. Only a portion of the gain (the inclusion rate) is added to your taxable income, making capital gains the most tax-efficient form of investment income.
